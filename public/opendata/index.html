How it is counted: each browser adds up what its visitor does and tells the game about once a minute. Only sums are kept — no names, no addresses, nothing that says who anybody is. A visitor is a browser: the same person on a phone and on a computer counts twice. Weeks start on Monday; days are those of the UTC calendar.

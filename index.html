In the near future, an AI started a nuclear war.

A handful of humans survived,
by moving their minds into small robots.

Of the whole planet,
only this valley and this beach were spared.

So have fun. Get some work done.
Take a walk. Enjoy the view.

Life goes by so fast anyway.
